## What is Kredex?

Traditional credit excludes billions of people because it demands collateral and a centralized credit score. **Kredex** replaces that with **on-chain reputation**: borrowers build creditworthiness through real repayment history, and lenders are protected by escrow contracts, dynamic risk models and automated default handling.

The product is **live on Stellar Testnet**. We are building the road to mainnet in the open, and we'd love your help.

## Contribute - for every level

- 🌱 **Complete beginner?** Fix a typo or clarify a doc in [docs](https://github.com/kredex-org/docs). Look for [`good first issue`](https://github.com/search?q=org%3Akredex-org+label%3A%22good+first+issue%22+state%3Aopen&type=issues).
- 🔧 **Comfortable with code?** Pick a [`help wanted`](https://github.com/search?q=org%3Akredex-org+label%3A%22help+wanted%22+state%3Aopen&type=issues) issue in product or contracts.
- 🦀 **Smart-contract or security expert?** Review the Soroban contracts and tell us what we missed. See our [security policy](https://github.com/kredex-org/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
- 💡 **Not a coder?** Share ideas, report bugs, test on Testnet, or help translate and write guides.

Read the **[Contributing Guide](https://github.com/kredex-org/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)**. It walks you through your first pull request step by step.
